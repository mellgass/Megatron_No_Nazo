Gameplay

One pilot.
Two forms.

Choose which of three satellites to capture first. Every capture earns an upgrade usable in both forms. The third crowns you King Starscream.

Fly the shoot ’em up approach and fight your way to the airlock.

Hardware concept

Lock on.

The goal is a physical cartridge for the original front-loading NES. A slim adapter holds the original Mystery of Convoy Famicom cartridge flat and flush with spring-loaded arms. A folding pack-in Transformer fills the slot for standalone play.

Standalone mode. Begin with the new satellite campaign.

Story

The game was rigged.

Megatron hides the Remote Control Circuit Linker cards from his TORQ III scheme inside copies of Mystery of Convoy. A human victory would trigger the takeover, but the game is so difficult that the plan stalls. Starscream pitches a fix. Capture three satellites and beam an update to Earth. Megatron sends Soundwave to assist over comms. Starscream has plans of his own.

Open classified transmission · Ending spoilers

Lock-on. After the first death in the original game, play switches to the satellite campaign. With all three satellites captured, you return as an invulnerable, transforming King Starscream with all three upgrades. Winning hands Starscream Earth’s broadcast network. He incapacitates Megatron, begins his takeover, and takes a seat on a throne made out of Megatron.

Standalone. King Starscream grins as he waits for the upload progress bar to fill. It never does.
